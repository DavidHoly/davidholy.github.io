Overview

Boden XR lets participants interact with high-fidelity 3D scans of typical Tyrolean soil profiles, mapping horizons and matching them to alpine altitudes. To make the soil "future-fit" against climate change, users can place various modifier cards on their soil and see the impacts of e.g earthworms or organic fertilizers in real time. Some measures have synergies with each other, visualizing the importance of making smart decisions when it comes to soil health.

Besides working on the project concept, creating the main gameplay sequence, and supporting the creation of the UI design, I helped to prepare the 3D scans of the soil samples for realtime rendering use.
