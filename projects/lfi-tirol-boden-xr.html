Overview

"Boden XR" is an interactive experience that allows users to analyze high-fidelity 3D scans of Tyrolean soil profiles across different alpine altitudes. Designed to highlight climate change adaptation, the app lets users place modifier cards, like organic fertilizers or earthworms, to observe real-time environmental impacts and discover positive synergies between sustainable practices.

I was involved in several aspects of this project, starting with shaping the overall concept and building the main gameplay sequence. On the visual side, I supported the UI design process, implemented the result, and supported the art team in preparing and optimizing the 3D soil scans for real-time rendering.
