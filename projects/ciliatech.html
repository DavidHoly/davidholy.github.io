Overview

The Ciliatech Intercil App showcases a special implant that helps to treat glaucoma while being less intrusive than other treatment options. The client wanted an app to bring to medical conferences to show off their new implant technology. To this end, we created various scenes revolving around a realistic model of a human eye. The user can click through various scenes showing different glaucoma cases and how the implant treatment works.

On this project I was tasked with setting up the various scenes with another technical artist. We implemented the eye 3D Model and set up different animation states using blend shapes. Additionally, I worked on implementing parts of the UI and created various visual effects and motion graphics to show things like damage and fluid flow in the eye.
