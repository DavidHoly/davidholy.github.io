Overview

Developed for use at medical trade shows and conferences, the Ciliatech Intercil iPad App demonstrates a novel, minimally invasive glaucoma treatment. Through interactive scenes featuring a realistic 3D human eye model, users can explore different glaucoma cases and see step-by-step how the implant functions inside the eye.

Working alongside another developer, I helped set up the interactive scenes and integrated the 3D eye model into the Unity project, using blend shape animations to show different scenarios and states of the eye. I also implemented UI elements and created animations and visual elements to clearly illustrate medical processes such as fluid flow and tissue damage inside the eye.
