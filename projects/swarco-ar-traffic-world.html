Overview

In the course of an extensive upgrade to the SWARCO TRAFFIC WORLD a new Augmented Reality application has been developed in order to add a new layer of information and bring the TRAFFIC WORLD‘s contents to life.

This was the first major project I worked on at MEDIASQUAD. I was tasked with creating the AR layer that would be projected over the real-life Traffic World environment. To that end, besides creating the app flow and integrating UI and 3D Assets, we visited SWARCO to measure the space and fit the 3D models to the environment. I also worked closely with our UI/UX artist and the design team from SWARCO to implement the information they wanted to convey to the visitors.

This project was rather challenging as the app had to run on an iPad while also being streamed to a large screen simultaneously. Furthermore the AR trackers had to be reworked multiple times to provide the accuracy we needed, which wasn't easy due to the highly reflective surfaces of the Traffic World room.
