Overview

Developed as part of a major overhaul to the SWARCO TRAFFIC WORLD exhibit, this Augmented Reality app overlays an interactive digital layer onto physical showroom exhibits. The application brings static content to life, giving visitors a look into SWARCO's smart traffic technology.

As my first major project at MEDIASQUAD, I was tasked with creating the 3D scenes that would be overlaid onto the real-life Traffic World exhibition space. I worked on the app flow, created animation sequences, integrated UI and 3D assets, and visited the Traffic World site to take measurements and map the 3D models accurately to the real environment. I also worked closely with our UI/UX artist and the design team from SWARCO to implement the information they wanted to convey to the visitors. Furthermore, I collaborated with the programmer of the AR tracking system, to accurately measure and place trackers on-site and test their accuracy.
