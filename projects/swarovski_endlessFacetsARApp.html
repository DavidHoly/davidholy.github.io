Overview

As part of the Career & Competence fair, SWAROVSKI expanded their exhibition stand by adding a virtual layer. Point the Endless Facets AR App at the booth’s info-walls, and various augmented features become visible through your smartphone or tablet. One point on the wall shows a real pop-up world map that contains all the companies. Other spots open info-boxes, quizzes or videos. All of them, of course, featuring the world-famous sparkle that SWAROVSKI is known for.

Key features

  • Instant physical booth expansion.
  • Very easy to use
  • Gamified career quizzes and media.
  • Swarovski's signature sparkling visual effects.
