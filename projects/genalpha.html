Overview

Commissioned by the University of Innsbruck, "Alpine Rush" is a mobile and web app that surveys children and teens on their winter vacation preferences through gamification. Players customize a virtual ski resort, unlocking bite-sized minigames between survey sections that tie directly into the resort areas they are developing.

On this project, my main focus was prototyping and polishing the minigames. Because each app session was limited to just a few minutes, the games needed to be intuitive and quick to pick up. We decided on simple, one-button mechanics focused on high scores. I created UI animations and particle effects to provide rewarding feedback to players. Beyond gameplay, I helped define the technical and modular asset requirements needed for the minigames, and handled UI layout and scene assembly in Unity.

Project Responsibilities

  • Asset Integration
  • UI Layout & Animations
