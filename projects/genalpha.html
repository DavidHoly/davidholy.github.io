Overview

"Gen Alpha Esports" is a mobile and web app comissioned by the University of Innsbruck. Intended for children and teens, the app lets users upgrade a ski resort to their liking, thereby surveying them on their activity preferences and wishes for their ideal ski-vacation. Between surveys, users are presented with minigames relating to the section of the resort they are upgrading.

For this project my main task was to create minigame prototypes and develop them into their final iteration. With a strict timelimit of only a few minutes per app-use, the games had to be short and easily understood. We landed on simple one button games that could not strictly be failed but worked on a high-score basis. To make gaining score more interesting, I created various UI animations and particle effects to play when a certain score threshold was reached.

In addition, I helped to develop the artstyle of the app and supported artists with the creation of the 3D Models for the minigames, keeping the target hardware in mind and focusing on optimization. Furthermore, I implemented the UI and audio.
