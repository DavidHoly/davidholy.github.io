Overview

Thüringer Bratwurst VR tasks players with saving a backyard party after a cheeky dog steals the Thüringer Bratwürste (Sausages). Users step into a butcher's shoes to grind the meat, mix spices, twist the casings, and grill to golden-brown perfection to earn the title of Bratwurst King. A toggleable recruitment layer adds testimonial videos from real butchers and guides the user to where to find active job listings, making Thüringer Bratwurst VR perfect to use at job fairs.

My role in this project was to create some of the sausage-making minigames. Besides this, I also created various visual effects and implemented a system that lets npcs react to player performance in the final minigame. I was also tasked with implementing 3D assets, npc animations, and audio as well as user interfaces.
