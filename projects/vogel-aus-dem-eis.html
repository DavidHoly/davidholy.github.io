Overview

"Vogel aus dem Eis" is a VR App that was comissioned by the medical university of Innsbruck to raise interest in the sciences as well as explore the possibilities of XR apps as a teaching tool. The app depicts the historic find of a purple heron mummy that was burried in a glacier over 350 years ago and lets players step into the shoes of a scientist conducting an examination of the remains.

In this project I had the opportunity to be the main technical artist, translating the project briefing into a development plan and coordinating tasks with other developers. I built sequences for every part of the examination. This included creating gameplay features, animations, and visual effects as well as coordinating with the UI/UX Artist on the implementation of the user interface and the information flow of the app.
