Overview

"Vogel aus dem Eis" is a VR educational experience commissioned by the Medical University of Innsbruck to raise interest in scientific study and showcase the potential of XR as an educational tool. The app brings to life the historic discovery of a 350-year-old mummified purple heron preserved in a glacier, placing users in the boots of a scientist examining the remains. Users are tasked with recovering the mummy from the ice and using various tools and devices to analyze the find, discovering science facts with every examination.

As the main Unity developer on this project, I was responsible for the in-engine implementation, translating the initial briefing into a structured development plan and coordinating tasks with the rest of the team. I built the examination sequences using internal sequencing tools and custom C# components to connect gameplay logic, 3D assets, animations, and audio. To guide users through the app, I then created various visual cues and effects to give players feedback.
