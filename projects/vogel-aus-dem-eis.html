Overview

Thanks to mixed reality, the laboratory comes directly into the classroom! The research journey begins with the discovery of a 350-year-old bird mummy, which is carefully documented, then uncovered, and finally scientifically examined. Key terms and scientific methods are playfully and impressively conveyed – making them truly memorable. Full attention guaranteed!
