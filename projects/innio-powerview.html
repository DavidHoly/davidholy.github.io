Overview

To showcase the new Type 6 Containerized Solution, INNIO Jenbacher commissioned the INNIO Powerview app. Users can interactively explore the highly detailed 3D model of the container, access technical data, and tap points of interest to receive additional information.

During development, I collaborated directly with the client's graphic design team to integrate their visual brand identity across the UI and 3D environment, using Illustrator to convert and their vector files into production-ready UI sprites and recreating mockups in Unity. I implemented the app flow and staged the 3D model in-engine, creating custom camera paths, baking the lighting, and setting up interactive points of interest with camera transitions, animations, visual highlights, and info overlays.
