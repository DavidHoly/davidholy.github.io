Overview

SWARCO Meta-City VR is a multi-user Virtual Reality platform that contextualizes SWARCO’s complete product portfolio within a digital city. Users act as the mayor or head engineer of the city and are prompted to solve various pain points, such as traffic congestion, air pollution, or pedestrian safety. Whilst the pain points are shown on a miniature map, the "Deep Dive" shows the user a life-sized intersection with their solution applied.

On this project, I prototyped layouts for the room environment and the miniature city, as well as implementing the customizable intersections and corresponding "Deep Dive" scenes. To that end, I created environment layouts and animation sequences, and worked closely with the team at SWARCO to convey product functionality and benefits to the user in an easy-to-understand way.
