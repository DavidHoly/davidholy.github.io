Overview

Every action counts. In BRP-Rotax VR Job Assessment, job applicants are beamed right to a virtual production line. Here, they can show their skill, dexterity, ability to learn quickly and their aptitude for the work in engine production. Thanks to high-end virtual reality, each step and each action feels incredibly real, almost as if you are right there at the production plant. Data analysis and evaluation is fully automated and unbiased, as it is based on participants' actual performance.

Key features

  • High-end graphics
  • Straightforward tutorial
  • Automated job evaluation
  • User management system
  • Motivation through gamification
