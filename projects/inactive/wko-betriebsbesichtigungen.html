Overview

Standing next to the roofer over the roofs of Vorarlberg. Going on an installation with the stove builder. Or take a look behind the scenes of a flower shop. With the virtual factory tours – the latest educational offensive of the Austrian Chamber of Commerce – this is made possible in a low-threshold, way thanks to VR headset and web app.

Developed in cooperation with our partner VRme.eu.

Key features

  • Video, photo and 360° VR content all in one app
  • Very easy to use
  • Innovative controls and presentation
