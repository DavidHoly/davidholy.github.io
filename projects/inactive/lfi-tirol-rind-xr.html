Overview

Rind XR is a multiuser Extended Reality application that allows up to ten participants — including an instructor — to witness calving first hand around a life-sized virtual cow. Before the delivery begins, students focus on choosing the neccesary equipment and preparing the virtual barn for the mother cow. Afterwards they learn about the different stages of calving, how they look like and how long each phase takes. While the birth itself is visual-only and focuses on timing and behavior, students must actively diagnose the situation — learning to identify critical birthing complications based on the visible position of the calf’s feet.

Key features

  • Highly engaging mixed reality (MR) learning experience for up to 10 people
  • Learn about the birth process of calfs and complications that could occur
  • high-end graphics
