Overview

The SWARCO Product Explorer is a multi-user Mixed Reality app designed to showcase SWARCO's expansive portfolio of traffic infrastructure technology. The application enables clients to interactively customize, test, and experience traffic technology products in a shared digital space.

My role in this project spanned concept creation and prototyping, app flow implementation, and integration of the various 3D products in-engine. Working alongside our art director, I created environment and interaction prototypes for the product browser to help narrow down the final feature set. I collaborated with the lead programmer, who wrote the network code and components, to create the interactive elements of the products and worked with the UI/UX artist to implement user interfaces for a multi-user mixed reality environment while sticking closely to the client's branding guidelines. Additionally, I integrated 3D products, created custom animations and VFX to demonstrate product functionality, and later implemented an update showcasing AI-assisted traffic control features.

  • Asset Integration & Animations
