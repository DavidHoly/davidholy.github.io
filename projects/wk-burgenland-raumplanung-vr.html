Overview

Developed for WKO Burgenland's Future Factory, this fast-paced VR app introduces teenagers to the world of interior design by challenging them to style their own virtual room in just three minutes. Players can select and place a variety of furniture, from beds and desks to dedicated fitness corners—and customize each item using different materials and colors.

As the main Unity developer on this project, I prototyped the room-customization mechanics, allowing players to place furniture and swap colors and materials. I coordinated with the rest of the team to define the required 3D assets and designed the vector UI graphics in Illustrator based on MEDIASQUAD standards and the client’s brand guidelines. Additionally, I handled the complete in-engine assembly, integrating the UI layout, 3D assets, audio, and gameplay logic for the final build.
