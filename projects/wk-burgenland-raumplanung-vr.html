Overview

How do you introduce teenagers to the creative world of interior design in a way that feels like pure play? Developed for WKO Burgenland's Future Factory, this fast-paced VR app gives young creators exactly three minutes to style their own virtual room. Players can choose and place a variety of beds, desks, lamps, and custom feature zones—like a dedicated fitness corner—using three distinct color variations for every object. It’s a rapid-fire design challenge that translates spatial planning into an addictive, hands-on gaming experience.

Key features

  • Insight into interior design as a career for children and teenager
  • Various design choices to mix and match
  • Very easy to use
  • Lively and fun sound design
