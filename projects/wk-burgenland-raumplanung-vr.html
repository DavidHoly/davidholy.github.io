Overview

Developed for WKO Burgenland's Future Factory to introduce teenagers to the world of interior design, this fast-paced VR app gives visitors exactly three minutes to style their own virtual room. Players can choose and place a variety of beds, desks, lamps, and custom feature zones—like a dedicated fitness corner—using three distinct color variations for every object.

As the main technical artist on this project I was responsible for planning the main gameplay loop and coordinating the needed 3D assets with the other artists. After creating the gameplay prototype, in which players can place furniture in different spots around a virtual room and change its color and material, I designed and implemented a user interface within the clients design guidelines and helped out in the creation and integration of 3D Assets.
