Overview

With "Wursttheke VR" Metzgerhandwerk Bayern aims to build interest at job fairs by putting teenagers behind the counter of a butcher's shop in a fast-paced, immersive virtual reality arcade game. Players must satisfy a parade of quirky, cartoonish customers by cutting precise portions of meat, slicing sausages, and serving up fresh local delicacies before the time runs out.

During this project, I built several minigames and implemented the ingredient delivery and scoring mechanics. Working alongside another developer, I integrated these features directly with their ordering system to finish the gameplay loop. Additionally, I assembled the user interfaces, prepared modular UI sprites, and created visual feedback, through particle effects and Shader Graph shaders, to guide players through the tutorial and make scoring feel rewarding.

Project Responsibilities

  • Sequence & Minigame Implementation
  • Asset Integration
  • UI Layout & Setup
  • Visual Feedback & Polish
  • Quality Assurance & Testing
