Overview

Commissioned by the Avalanche Warning Service Tyrol, "Notfall Lawine VR" simulates a high-stakes avalanche rescue scenario in a safe, accessible, and repeatable format. We were tasked with designing a training experience that teaches players the fundamentals of avalanche safety and emergency response when someone is buried. Unlike traditional mountain training, VR can be set up instantly and repeated as needed. By leveraging VR's immersion, an ever-present timer, and meaningful player choices, the app recreates the real emotional stakes and pressure of an actual rescue mission.

For this project, I was involved in planning core gameplay concepts and creating prototype environment layouts. My main focus was on building the garage tutorial level in Unity, guiding players and teaching basic mechanics. Additionally, I modeled and textured supporting 3D props used in both the garage and mountain environments.
