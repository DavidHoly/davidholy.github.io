Overview

Experience a companion rescue after an avalanche in a realistic, immersive but completely safe way: Our simulator accompanies the user step by step, from the selection of equipment to the actual search process. A special feature: mistakes can be made at any time – their often fatal consequences can be experienced directly through the app, which significantly increases the learning effect and awareness for the topic.

Key features

  • Engaging story
  • Gamified learning experience
  • Randomized content
  • High-end graphics
  • Different difficulty levels
  • Helps to save lives!
