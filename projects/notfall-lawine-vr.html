Overview

Comissioned by the Avalance Warning Service Tirol, the "Notfall Lawine VR" App simulates an avalance rescue scenario in a safe, accessible, and repeatable way. We were tasked with creating a training that teaches players the basics of avalanche safety and what to do when someone is burried. The benefit of the VR training is twofold: It can be setup quickly and used repeatedly as opposed to traditional training on a mountain. And, by leveraging the immersion factor of VR, we can recreate some of the heightened emotions and stakes that would be present in an actual emergency. This feeling is further strengthened by an everpresent timer and the choices and mistakes the user can make while packing their gear and carrying out the rescue.

In this project I helped to plan gameplay concepts, create prototype environment layouts and was responsible for integrating assets and implementing the garage tutorial. I was also tasked with creating some of the 3D Assets seen in the garage and on the mountain.
