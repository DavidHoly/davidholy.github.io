Overview

The Novartis VR Tour lets users explore the three Novartis locations in Austria and get insight into the history of the company. Through 360° videos and 3D scenes, users can take a peek into the production lines of a modern pharmaceutical company.

This virtual tour is one of many projects I worked on that combine 360° Videos with 3D content in a VR app. It was my job to coordinate the video integration with SchubertTV and to plan and implement app flow and UI design together with the UI/UX artist, making sure to stick to the company branding guidelines of the client. In addition I animated the introductory sequence.
