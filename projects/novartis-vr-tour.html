Overview

The Novartis VR Tour allows users to explore Novartis's three primary locations in Austria and gain insight into the company's rich history. Through a blend of 360° videos and interactive 3D environments, visitors get an exclusive look inside the production facilities of a modern pharmaceutical company.

This project is one of several I’ve worked on that combine 360° video with interactive 3D content in VR. I handled video integration in close coordination with SchubertTV, and collaborated with our UI/UX artist to plan and build the overall app flow and interface while strictly adhering to Novartis's corporate branding guidelines. Additionally, I built and animated the intro sequence that welcomes users to the experience.

Project Responsibilities

  • App flow & Sequence Implementation
  • Video & Asset Integration
  • UI Layout & Setup
  • Quality Assurance & Testing
