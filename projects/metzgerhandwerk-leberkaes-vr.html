Overview

In "Leberkäse VR" users get to create their own meatloaf in a virtual butchers shop. Metzgerhandwerk Bayern aims to build interest in the profession at job fairs. Users can playfully experiment with different ingredients and create different types of meatloafs (Leberkäse).

In this project I implemented everything revolving around the main mixing and cooking mechanic. I received a prototype setup by our programmer and swapped out placeholder UI and created visual feedback through animations and visual effects. Finally, I implented the game into a larger gameplay sequence with a tutorial and scoring UI depending on the mix of ingredients chosen by the player.
