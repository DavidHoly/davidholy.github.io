Overview

The Novartis Virtual Sitetour is a WebGL application that maps out the entire Novartis Schaftenau manufacturing campus as an interactive 3D model. Users can choose from four tours that combine 360° video tracks with interactive hotspots. The virtual tour grants access to usually restricted environments like cleanrooms and state-of-the-art drug production lines. Integrated points of interest scattered throughout the experience allow users to pause and receive additional content like explanations, videos, or minigames.

When I joined MEDIASQUAD, the first two tours of this application already existed. When we got the contract to add an additional two tours, I was tasked with coordinating the implementation together with our UI/UX artist and technical artist. After analyzing how the original tours were structured, we made a project plan and started implementing the videos recorded for us by SchubertTV. Additionally, I placed the points of interest in each scene according to the wishes of the client. These points of interest included videos, text popups, or minigames. Finally, I helped to create 3D representations of the new buildings for the overview campus model and the in-tour minimap.
