Overview

SWARCO VR Training allows for onboarding training in a safe and repeatable environment. By recreating common traffic controller maintenance tasks in VR, the trainee can practice the operation of the ITC-3 traffic controller in a hands-on way without the need for a real intersection. During a module, the user will learn the ins and outs of traffic controller components and be asked to solve common maintenance tasks. This knowledge is then strengthened by an exam at the end of every module.

On this ongoing project, I supported the primary development team by setting up tutorial gameplay sequences, assembling UI layouts, and performing quality assurance for the current build. Working on an active enterprise training application gave me valuable hands-on experience structuring step-by-step onboarding flows and guided maintenance procedures in VR.

Project Responsibilities

  • UI Layout & Setup
