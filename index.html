About

Hi, I'm David!

I'm a multidisciplinary creative with a passion for creating characters and bringing them to life.

With over six years of experience across product design and front-end development, I help teams turn complex ideas into clear, accessible, and delightful user experiences. I care deeply about typography, performance, and the small details that make products memorable.

When I'm not designing or coding, you'll find me exploring local coffee shops, sketching in my notebook, or contributing to open-source UI libraries.
