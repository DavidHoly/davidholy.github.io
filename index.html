About

Hi, I'm David!

I'm a Unity Developer & Technical Artist based in Vienna with a passion for interactive experiences.

During my last position at the award winning XR studio MEDIASQUAD I worked on many VR/AR projects, gaining a strong interest in immersive learning and gamification. I worked on both the technical and visual side of development, helping to ship many enterprise training and serious game projects. During my day to day I supported designers and programmers by integrating assets, creating visuals, implementing features, and managing design documentation.

I strongly believe in the power of learning through play and love the creative process of collaborating with a team to create projects with real-world impact.
